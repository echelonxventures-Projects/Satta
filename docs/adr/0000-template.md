# ADR-0000: Template

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Rejected
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes**: ADR-NNNN, or none
- **Superseded by**: ADR-NNNN, or none

## Context

What forced a decision. Include the constraint that made the obvious option unavailable —
that is usually the part a reader cannot reconstruct later.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice. One decision per ADR.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, and what it makes hard. List the cost explicitly. If no cost can be
named, the decision has probably not been understood yet.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. Include options rejected for non-technical
reasons — budget, licensing, staffing — because those reasons expire and the option may
become viable again.

## Invariant impact

Which of the 12 safety invariants this touches, and whether it strengthens, preserves or
weakens each. A decision that weakens one is a defect unless the weakening is explicit,
bounded and accepted here.
