# Delivery Realities & Constraints

These govern whether the plan succeeds. State them plainly.

## Timeline

- **Launch slice**: 12-18 months with 25-40 engineers plus compliance, legal, finance and trading staff
- **Full 52 tasks**: Multi-year programme (3-5 years), 100+ engineer range
- **No shortcut exists**: There is no version of this that a small team ships quickly

## Licensing (Parallel Track)

- Licence application, corporate structuring, banking and PSP onboarding, and RNG plus game-math certification take 6-12 months
- These run IN PARALLEL with engineering, not after
- Engineering completion without them means a platform that cannot legally take a bet
- Default target jurisdiction: Malta MGA (credible, EU passporting). Alternatives: Curacao or Anjouan (faster, cheaper, less prestige)

## Certification

- In regulated markets only lab-approved builds may run
- This shapes the release process from the start — certified-build attestation is in Task 49
- Not an afterthought; it's structural

## Tax & Jurisdiction Rules

- These are ongoing content, not a one-time build
- They need a named owner, legislative monitoring per market, and a release cadence
- Treating them as shipped code is how operators discover material underpayments during audit

## External Dependencies (Cannot Build)

- Assurance opinions
- Lab certifications
- Licences
- Market-specific tax and gaming-law advice
- All require independent qualified third parties. The platform makes them efficient; it does not replace them.

## The Gating Decision

**Market selection** is the highest-leverage decision available. Each additional jurisdiction multiplies compliance, tax, reporting and certification work. Sequence markets deliberately.
